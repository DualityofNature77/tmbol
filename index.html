Idara Whem is the author of The Modern Day Book of Life and the architect of the Sacred Balance system. She founded Sacred Balance Library, an imprint of Sieber Fillman Publishing, and created the first Kemetic Alignment Practitioner certification built entirely within the tradition.

Questions

Is this a religion?

No. It's a daily practice and an ethical framework. You don't need any particular faith to use it.

Is this divination or fortune-telling?

No. The Sacred Balance system includes no divination, prophecy, or prediction. Nile signs describe an archetype and a practice, not a fate.

Do I need to buy all thirteen volumes?

No. Start with your own sign. Many readers add the volumes of a partner, child, or friend.

My sign's volume isn't out yet. What should I read first?

Start with The Modern Day Book of Life. Every Nile volume builds on it. Join the list above and you'll get an email the day your volume is released.
